Initializing the Lab — Bridging Medical Physics & AI

Why I’m Building in Public:

Welcome to my digital logbook!

My background sits at a fascinating intersection: I hold a Master’s in Medical Physics and an Advanced Certificate in Data Science. In clinical medicine, precision isn't just a metric but it directly impacts patient outcomes. Every radiation dose curve, DICOM voxel, and anatomical contour carries real human weight.

As machine learning and computer vision rapidly reshape healthcare, my goal is to build technology that deeply understands both sides of the equation: the underlying physical principles of medical imaging and radiation transport, alongside the intelligence of modern neural networks.

In my logbooks, I intend to document:

  • Theoretical Foundations: Core concepts and principles in medical physics and data science.
  • My Projects: Dose-volume histograms (DVHs), DICOM processing, and 3D organ segmentation tools.
  • Research & Field Notes: Paper breakdowns, algorithms, and vision models in radiation oncology.
  • Project Chronicles: Step-by-step documentation as I build and benchmark healthcare AI pipelines.
